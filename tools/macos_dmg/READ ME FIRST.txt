KeeperFX for Apple Silicon (unofficial fork)
============================================

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 15 or later) and your own copy of
Dungeon Keeper or Dungeon Keeper Gold (GOG, EA, Steam, or the original CD).
The original game files can't be included here.

1. Drag the "KeeperFX" folder from this disk image to somewhere on your Mac
   (Applications, Documents, Desktop - anywhere you can write to).

2. Open Terminal and clear the "downloaded from the internet" flag once
   (the app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized). Type this, a space, then drag the
   KeeperFX folder into the Terminal window, and press Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine

3. In the KeeperFX folder, double-click "Add Dungeon Keeper Files.command" and
   drag your Dungeon Keeper folder into the window when asked. (GOG: use the
   files inside its CD image - mount or extract it first.)

4. Open KeeperFX.app. If macOS asks for access to the folder, click Allow.

Your saves, settings and screenshots go in ~/Library/Application Support/KeeperFX.
keeperfx.cfg is an optional starter config; the game works without editing it.

Saves from builds before macos-v1.5.0 won't load.

This is an unofficial personal fork with no support. The game itself:
https://github.com/dkfans/keeperfx   This fork: https://github.com/matthewdeaves/keeperfx
